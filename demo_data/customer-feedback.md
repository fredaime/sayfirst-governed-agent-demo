# Customer feedback digest — July/August 2026

Fourteen interviews, three segments. Verbatim themes below.

**Insurance (5 interviews).** Every one raised the same blocker: an agent that
can send correspondence to a policyholder cannot be deployed without a human in
the loop, and bolting approval onto the prompt "does not survive an audit". One
head of claims: "I do not need the model to be careful. I need it to be unable."

**Logistics (4 interviews).** Less regulated, more impatient. They want
automatic execution for read and internal-write actions and human approval only
for anything that leaves the building. Two asked specifically whether a denied
action is recorded or silently dropped.

**Public sector (5 interviews).** Slowest cycle, sharpest questions. Three
asked what happens when the governance service is unreachable. The expected
answer — the action does not happen — was not what they assumed, and one said
that answer alone would move us to the shortlist.

Cross-cutting: nobody asked for better dashboards. Everyone asked who decides.
