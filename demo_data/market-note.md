# Market note — Q3 2026, EU edge-inference segment

Three things moved this quarter.

**Pricing.** Per-token pricing for hosted small models fell 34% quarter over
quarter. Two vendors now publish sub-cent pricing for 4B-class models. Our own
hosted margin assumption (62%) no longer holds for the hosted tier; the edge
tier is unaffected because it bills per-appliance.

**Regulation.** The EU AI Act's transparency obligations for general-purpose
models entered application in August. Buyers in regulated sectors now ask for
an audit trail of automated actions during procurement, not after. Four of our
last six RFPs contained an explicit "who authorised this action" question.

**Competition.** Two incumbents shipped agent frameworks with built-in tracing.
Neither ships an authorisation boundary: their audit trail records what the
agent did, after it did it. That is the gap we sell into.

Recommendation: hold hosted pricing, lead every regulated-sector conversation
with the authorisation boundary rather than with the tracing.
